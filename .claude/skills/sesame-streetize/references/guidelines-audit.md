# Audit of the four Sesame pages against the license guidelines (2026-11-13)

Pages: `/explorations/sesame-toddlers-probiotic`, `-multi`, `-bundle`, `-bts`. Rules in `sesame-street/references/license-guidelines.md`. What follows is what to change, in priority order. Status (2026-11-19): **applied to the Toddlers' Probiotic page and to the Toddlers' Multi page** (`/explorations/sesame-toddlers-multi`: original first with the "Choose The Edition" picker through `SLOT_AFTER_QTY` of MainProductV2, `SesameToddlersMultiStyled.jsx` is the edition, generic lines in `StickersMulti.jsx` and `GANG`, `CountSection friends={false}`, closing without a character, Limited Edition strip, `ss-legal`, the street scene `StreetScroll` with its own `copy` and `pictures` props); the other two pages (bundle, Behind the Science) wait for Sesame's approval and then get the same changes. Page flow of every page: original first, picker, Abby's spell, the edition. How it was done on the Probiotic page: generic lines in `Stickers.jsx` and `Fx.jsx` (default mascot lines), `GANG` lines and `scaled` Gang in the page, `Wholesome friends={false}` and `CountSection friends={false}`, headings rewritten, closing without a character ("A Happy Tummy Journey Starts Here", "Hello, neighbor!"), no Big Bird on Add To Cart (CSS), no celebration on add to cart, the Count counter says "One!", "Two!", the logo strip is white, no clouds, 12 ducks, no lamp post (street sign), legal notice block (`ss-legal`), `noindex` meta and header. Still open: the mixed art styles question and the photo-vs-illustration decision, the pupils check of every sticker, and the Sesame review itself.

## A. Must change (rule broken)
1. **Character lines about the product.** Characters may not name the product, claim, promote a benefit or say anything about First Day. Rewrite every bubble to a catchphrase or generic true-to-character phrase. Lines to remove or replace (examples): "Big Bird is on the bottle!", "Elmo found the gut helper!", "Elmo is on this bottle!", "Elmo found Big Bird on the bottle!", "Hello! Pre, pro and post. That is three in one!", "Hello! Vitamins C and D3 are on the team!", "Super Grover says the right dose for tiny growers!", "Ten vitamins! ...", "Only 2g! ...", "Three strains!", "Five reasons!", "Two gummies a day!", "Fake sugars? Hmph! Not here.", "No fake sugars? Hmph! Nothing to grouch about.", "Happy gut, happy tummy. Me know!", "Golden Kiwi flavor! La la la!", "Big Bird counts six!", "Big Bird counts 21 superfoods!", "Elmo says thank you, veggies!", the Gang lines (they mention bottles, vitamins, strains) and the Elmo mascot lines ("Two gummies a day. Grown-ups give them!", "Tap the Sesame Street sign. Elmo has a surprise!"). Keep or write only: "Elmo loves you!", "Hello!", "Thank you!", the Count counting "One, two, three! Ah ah ah!", Cookie "Me love cookies!" / "Om nom nom", Oscar grouchy about everyday things (never about the product), Grover "Hello! It is I, Grover!".
2. **Characters on the CTA / closing.** The Big Bird head on "Add To Cart & View Gift" and on the hero CTA of the landing is a character giving a CTA (and the head is a cut neck that floats). Remove it from every button. The closing "Big Bird (or Elmo) Is Ready For A Happy Growing Journey, Are You?" + "Order Today" is a character inviting to buy: rewrite the closing as a First Day headline with no character speaker, characters out of the hero, only the logo.
3. **Headings where a character speaks or endorses.** "Oscar Looked For Junk And Found None", "Oscar Checked Every Multi, So Why Should You Choose First Day?", "Smooth, Fruity, Sweet And Natural, Oscar Has Nothing To Grouch About", "The Count Counted The Sugar, Up To 75% Less Than Others", "The Count Counted It! 93 Out Of 100 Toddlers...", "Grover Wants To Raise The Bar On Toddler Nutrition", "Big Bird Is On The Bottle And 21 Organic Superfoods Are Inside", "Elmo Is On The Bottle And Pre, Post And Probiotic Are Inside", "Big Bird Wonders: ...". Keep the play on the show with words and neutral references ("Neighbor", "Sunny Day", counting, "Rubber Duckie") and drop the character as the subject of a product claim.
4. **Count section and counters.** The Count counting First Day's own stats (10 vitamins, 21 superfoods, 2g of sugar) is a character promoting product benefits. Present the numbers as First Day's, with the Count only as decoration doing generic counting, or drop the character from the stat cards. The quantity counter ("Two bottles! Ah ah ah!") is generic counting and fine if it only counts.
5. **Characters beside the product picture.** Stickers must not hold or touch the bottle: check every sticker on the lifestyle and nutrition pictures and on the wheel (none may overlap the bottle). Real Wholesome (a character under a claim icon with the claim text) reads as the character promoting benefits: separate the icon + claim from the character or use generic art.
6. **Legal notice missing.** Add "TM and © 2026 Sesame Workshop." (or the medium/long form) in the legal block of each page.
7. **Meet The Gang is not to scale.** The row uses almost the same height for everyone. Use the scale chart (Big Bird tallest, Cookie about 2/3, Count mid, Grover, Elmo, Abby smaller) and pupils of similar size.
8. **Never cropped pupils / awkward crops.** Review stickers that peek from the edge of pictures or are clipped by the wrapper (Elmo in the wheel, feet of Big Bird were cut, heads in the round icons cut at the neck: acceptable for avatars only if the pupils are whole and the circle is clean). The `bigbird-head.svg` (neck paths removed) is an altered, floating head: retire it.
9. **Mixed art styles.** The bottle packaging is photographic (live-action Big Bird and Elmo plush) and the stickers are House Style illustration (Version B). "Never mix styles": ask Sesame how to treat the packaging, or reduce illustration to a few flat stickers.
10. **Video script** (hero placeholder of the landing): do not recreate the 123 street sign or the street as a set (use the 2D logo as a bug), the Count may not explain nutrients or labels (CHOLINE, B6...), Big Bird next to the bottle may not touch it, Cookie Monster biting a broccoli is fine as a generic moment, the models and clothes carry no brand logos, no song or voice (already so).

## B. Should change (reads against the spirit)
- **Clutter**: the pages stack a lot (clouds, confetti, rubber duckies, Real Wholesome with 8 characters, Gang, Count, mascot, Tango). "Clean and modern; crowded layouts read as dated". Keep one lead character per section, drop confetti and clouds, keep the mascot optional.
- **"Sesame Street Edition" strip and logo**: OK as the 2D logo; keep clear space (half the logo height) and at least 50px wide; do not put it on a yellow strip that competes with its own yellow border (use white or the sky blue); never next to a character line that sounds like an endorsement.
- **Fixed Elmo mascot** says things about the product and "Tap the Sesame Street sign": either remove or use only "Elmo loves you!" style lines.
- **Cookie Monster eating**: allowed (he is canonical) but never next to the gummy or the bottle, and the "sometimes food" sentence is a health statement in his mouth: drop it.
- **Count that "Counts 30, 60 And 90 Days"** (the timeline heading) attributes the page's promises to a character: reword to a neutral heading.

## C. Process
- **Approval**: all concepts and materials go to Sesame Workshop BEFORE they are made or posted (3 to 5 business days; the deck of this audit can be the submission). Our Vercel link is public: add `noindex` (meta tag and an `X-Robots-Tag` header in `vercel.json`) and password protect the deployment, and share it internally only until approval.
- **Questions to put to Sesame** (guidelines say they are "open to discussion"): can the stickers sit near, not on, the packaging photo; can the Count count generic numbers beside First Day's numbers; can a headline name a character as a play on words; is a Sesame Street edition template with a "Sesame Street Edition" strip approved; which logo version is on the bottle (2D or 3D, must be the same in marketing).
