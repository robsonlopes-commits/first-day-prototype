## Lessons for design work
- Teens and Kids are the traffic audiences: they are the right first demos for any new page (see the demo rule in `firstday-design`).
- The Sale page and the PDPs convert; education LPs and advertorials lose people. When building a new landing concept (such as the All-in-1 bundle), borrow the conversion elements: a visible offer, the buy box early, proof next to the CTA, short blocks.
- The Behind the Science LP template (kde, tdk, trmv) is shared and content-driven: one layout, per audience content.
