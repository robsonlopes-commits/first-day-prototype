1. **The wrong form:** cheap synthetic forms that pass through; up to 40% of kids cannot convert synthetic folic acid.
2. **The wrong dose for the wrong body:** a 3 year old and a 14 year old dosed identically, scaled down from an adult formula.
3. **Formulated for a label, not an environment:** nothing built for vanishing nutrients, microplastics and additives blocking magnesium.
