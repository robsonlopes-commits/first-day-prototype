# Video (owner rules, 2026-10-17): always the cheapest model, never audio, 10 to 15 seconds

Moved out of SKILL.md on 2026-11-11 (video is rarely needed). Same rules as before.

- Script `node tools/generate-video.mjs --prompt "..." --image first-frame.jpg --seconds 4|6|8 --out clip.mp4` always uses **Veo 3.1 Lite** (`veo-3.1-lite-generate-preview`, 720p, about $0.05 per second, so $0.20 to $0.40 per clip). It prints the estimate before sending. Never use the Standard, Fast or Omni models unless the owner asks. Lite clips are 4, 6 or 8 seconds and Lite cannot extend a clip, so a 10 to 15 second video is **3 or 4 clips stitched** (about $0.70 for 14 seconds).
- **No audio:** the API always renders an audio track for Veo and there is no switch for it. `tools/stitch-video.swift` (compile once: `swiftc -O tools/stitch-video.swift -o /tmp/stitch`; `stitch out.mp4 trim|square|none clip1.mp4 clip2.mp4 ...`) copies ONLY the video tracks, so the final file has none (check with an AVFoundation track count). There is no ffmpeg on this machine, the stitcher uses macOS AVFoundation.
- **Black bars:** every Veo 720p clip has 3 black pixel rows at the top and 3 at the bottom (found 2026-10-17 as a thin black line above and below the hero video). Always stitch with `trim` (removes 4 rows top and bottom, 1280x712, re-encodes H.264 at about 2.5 Mbps, about 4 MB for 14 s). `square` also center-crops to 1:1. `none` is passthrough and keeps the bars: do not use it for pages. After exporting, check the edge rows of a few frames are bright (not about 5 of 255).
- Method: write the storyboard first, make one key frame per shot with the image model (16:9, the people of the page as `--ref` so they stay the same), then animate each key frame (image to video) with a short prompt of what moves. Look at the frames of the result by loading the mp4 in the Browser pane and drawing frames on a canvas. Keep files small (about 4 MB for 14 seconds).
- Story structure approved for the "problem, cause, solution" video: (1) the problem happening to the child or teen, (2) a stylized zoom inside the body showing low nutrients (soft glowing particles, nothing scary, no text), (3) the child or teen takes the gummy and the problem eases. Mood only: never show or promise a change in height or a medical result.
- Cost rule of the organization: new spend is allowed up to $100 per month when pre-approved by Alice, Drew, Jerry, Dan, Keith or Yoed; keep a running total and report it.
