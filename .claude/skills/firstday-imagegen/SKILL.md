# First Day image generation

Script: `npm run image -- --prompt "..." --out <name> [--ref <png> ...] [--aspect 16:9] [--size 1K] [--model lite|pro] [--dry-run]`
Default model `gemini-nano-banana-2.1`; keep it. `--model lite` is the cheapest but is not built for reference images. `--model pro` (`gemini-3-pro-image`) was tested on 2026-11-11: about $0.20 per 2K image, better light and perspective, but it ignores sizes (bottles 2 to 3 times too big) and sometimes invents a different label, so it is NOT better for bottles. Output goes to `public/assets/custom/<name>.jpg` (the API only returns JPEG), the prompt is logged in `public/assets/custom/PROMPTS.md`, and the page uses it with `customImg('<name>.jpg')` from `src/lib/assets.js`.
Older recipes: `references/bottle-recipes.md` (full-scene lid passes and lid patches, for scenes with SEVERAL bottles) and `references/video.md` (Veo clips).

## Setup, cost and allowed content
- Key in `.env.local` as `GEMINI_API_KEY=...` (gitignored; the repo is public). Never print, read back or commit the key. If the script says it is missing, stop and tell the user.
- The script prints the estimated cost per image. The owner pre-approved the generation a task needs (the organization allows up to $100 per month of new spend pre-approved by Alice, Drew, Jerry, Dan, Keith or Yoed); keep a running count and report the total. Use 1K for new scenes with people, 2K for edits where a bottle is small in the frame. Re-use existing images when they fit (`public/assets/shop`, `public/assets/custom`, the production pages).
- The owner uses AI images to explore concepts BEFORE paying for photo shoots. Approved without asking: children and families in everyday moments (including eating or holding gummies), AI product images (pass the real cutouts of `public/assets/shop` as `--ref` and ask to keep the label exactly), short correctly spelled text inside the image, and any scene that serves the story.

## The real bottle (put this in every prompt that shows one)
- **Size: exactly 4.72 inches tall and 2.95 inches wide (12 cm by 7.5 cm).** Models follow inches better than centimeters. Ratio height : width = 1.6 : 1 (8 : 5), the width is 62.5 percent of the height; a bottle as wide as it is tall, or wider, is wrong. Write: "a standing cylinder 4.72 inches (12 cm) tall and 2.95 inches (7.5 cm) wide, clearly taller than wide, ratio 8 to 5".
- **Shape of the top.** The upper part is ONE continuous band (the band behind the first day logo: white with a faint cool tint on the standard bottles, pale blue on the Toddlers Sesame edition) that rises to the top edge. There is NO separate tall cap block above it. The lid is a very thin, perfectly flat, closed disc level with the top edge, the same color as the band, with one hairline edge: no rim, no lip, no recess, no hole, no inner circle. On it, a small blue line-art First Day bird in the center and a thin ring of tiny blue uppercase text near the edge, printed flat and squashed by the perspective. The model's usual mistakes: a tall white cap, a lid that looks sunken like a dish, a tinted lid, a bottle that looks squat.
- The real cutouts in `public/assets/shop` (and the Sesame bottle `bottle1000.png`) are front views, so the model invents the top: give it the lid references. Kept locally in `reference/bottle-photos/` (gitignored, they show a personal desk, never commit): `bottle-in-hand.webp`, `lid-top-from-above.webp`, `correct-lid-kids-bedtime.jpg`, `correct-lid-teens-school.jpg`, `correct-lid-zoom.jpg`. If missing, ask the owner for a real photo of the bottle and of the lid from above.

## Putting ONE bottle in a scene: the exact-size method (owner approved the sizes, 2026-11-11)
Asking the model to draw a bottle at a given size does not work (2.1: 1.5 to 2 times too big, even with inches, percent and two-step edits; Pro: worse). So the size is set by compositing and the model only integrates:
1. **Size from a reference object with a researched real size.** Pick one or two rigid objects of the scene at about the SAME DISTANCE from the camera as the bottle. Look up the real size on the web (a product page, not a blog; write the source in `PROMPTS.md`). Measure the object in the image (% of the image height) and convert: bottle height in image = object height in image x 4.72 in / object real height. Cross-check with a second object and take the middle value; AI scenes are not always in scale (a duck can look big next to a towel), so if two objects disagree by more than 1.5 times, take the middle and look at it. Researched sizes: Munchkin Click Lock Flip Straw Cup 9 oz, 7.21 in tall x 2.66 in wide (Target A-83705453); 12 oz beverage glass 4 to 6 in tall, about 3 in wide (Anchor Hocking 7732U: 4 7/8 x 3 3/8 in); classic rubber duck about 3.5 in (Schylling); hand towel 16 x 30 in, tri-fold about 0.5 to 0.75 in thick (estimate); head of a 3 year old 7.5 to 8.5 in (weak source, use with a second object); bowl 6 to 7 in wide; tote bag about 14 in tall. Result on the Sesame page: kitchen (sippy cup) 31% of the image height, boy (bowl) 30%, berries (glass) 27% to 36%, tote 20% to 26%, bathroom 10% (duck) to 27% (towels), middle 18%; the owner confirmed the sizes were good.
2. **Remove the old product** with an edit from the ORIGINAL scene: "completely remove the stand-up pouch ... fill the space ... keep the exact same framing, crop and zoom, everything else unchanged". Use the default 2.1 (it kept the framing, Pro re-framed two scenes); compare with the original.
3. **Paste the real cutout** at that size with `tools/place-product.swift` (compile once: `swiftc -O tools/place-product.swift -o /tmp/place`; `/tmp/place base.jpg bottle.png out.jpg <cx> <cy> <heightPx> 0 ffffff`; the bottle comes out at the right 0.62 width to height ratio, label pixel exact). Base of the bottle on the surface, a little higher in the frame if it stands further back.
4. **One integration pass** (2.1) with refs: 1) the composite, 2) the real front view, 3 to 5) lid references. Prompt: "Make a strict, minimal, pixel aligned edit that ONLY makes the bottle look photographed in this scene, not cut out and pasted: the thin elliptical flat lid top (no tall white cap) and the elliptical base matching the camera angle of the other round objects, a soft natural contact shadow, the light direction, color and softness of the scene, subtle reflections of the room on the label. Keep the bottle's EXACT size, position, ratio 8 to 5, label design and colors; do not resize, move or redraw it. Do not change anything else." 2 variants, keep the one that did not resize the bottle. Never let the integration pass run from a previous result.
5. **Fix the small print** with `tools/label-patch.sh base.jpg out.jpg bottle.png:cx:cy` (env `LP_PCT` = crop width in percent of the image width, about 0.94 x bottle height / image width x 100; needs `/tmp/blend` from `tools/blend-patch.swift`). Retry once if a word is wrong.
6. **QA at full size** (below), then save downscaled: `sips -Z 1600 -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 88 in.jpg --out public/assets/custom/<name>.jpg`; keep the big originals outside the repo.
Typical bottle size in 4:5 people photos (measured on the approved All-in-1 photos): 12 to 15% of the image width and 18 to 22% of the height, about the height of a drinking glass.
**Perspective, light and shadow must match the scene** (a bottle that looks cut out in a paint program is rejected): same camera angle as the surface, same light direction and color, contact shadow, grounded base ellipse like the other round objects.
**Infographics (gallery slides) that show a bottle:** the model cannot reproduce small print. Generate and approve one layout, remove the bottle with an edit, edit copy and icons on the empty layout, and paste the real cutout with `tools/place-product.swift` (`auto` picks the circle tint). Pattern `aio-info-*`: pale background, tag, two-line navy and purple headline, three bordered cards with icon chips, legal line, floating navy footer bar; keep everything inside a 5% safe margin.

## QA gates (MANDATORY, never ship an image you did not look at)
- **Every bottle: crop the lid and the label at full resolution** (`sips -c <h> <w> --cropOffset <y> <x> img.jpg --out crop.jpg`, y first and then x, then Read it). Lid passes only if: one uniform face with no darker, greyer or bluer inner ellipse, no thick raised rim or step, level with its edge, bird and text ring flat and squashed by the perspective, no hole. Label: words spelled right (a few tiny letters may stay wrong, say so). If the lid fails, generate variations or use `references/bottle-recipes.md` (lid patch). Also check the ratio (taller than wide) and the size against the objects.
- **Images with people or objects:** count people, heads, arms (2), hands (2), fingers (5), legs; faces symmetric; no extra, fused or melted limbs or objects; no garbled text or logo. The picture must make sense on its own (the object matches the action; a negative shows the problem, the positive the relief; a pair keeps the same people, room and framing and changes only what was asked). Write the checklist of what must be true and mark pass or fail. If anything fails, generate 3 to 4 alternatives instead of lowering the bar, and say in the summary which variant was kept.
- A model editing a photo will not shrink a person or an object: describe the posture or regenerate the scene, or composite.

## People scenes
- A page with several moments shows a DIFFERENT person or family in each (age within the audience, ethnicity, hair, build, clothes, room); name them up front in the prompt ("a two year old Hispanic boy and his mother", ...). The positive and negative photo of the SAME moment keep the same people.
- Style block that gave the approved photos: 1K, 4:5 (or 1:1, 4:3), a close candid composition with one to four people, "Photorealistic editorial family photography, soft natural window light, warm and bright, airy white and light blue palette with subtle cobalt blue accents, shallow depth of field, candid and dignified, natural realistic faces and hands." and "Gummies are small soft star shaped gummies". Do not paste flat cutouts or run 2K or 4K whole-scene passes for new scenes with people (the Family Box Builder images were rejected for that). Read the prompts of the images the owner liked in `PROMPTS.md` first.
- Negative "before" twin of an existing photo: pass the happy photo as `--ref`, ask for a strict pixel-aligned edit (same crop, zoom, people and objects; remove the bottle and gummies; change only expressions and body language). No hand left pinching or holding nothing; every hand rests naturally. Make the negative clearly but dignifiedly exaggerated (really bored, really tired, restless). Always Read the hands.

## Typography inside generated images: Sofia Pro only, NEVER Gelica
Every word drawn inside an AI image must look like **Sofia Pro**: a clean geometric sans-serif. No Gelica, serif, slab, script or rounded-serif, even in small card titles. Put in EVERY prompt with text: "All text is set in a clean geometric sans-serif typeface like Sofia Pro (regular and bold weights only). Absolutely no serif fonts, no Gelica, no slab, no script, no decorative type." Reject and regenerate any result with a serif. For claims and wording read `firstday-brand-knowledge` (approved claims only).

## Guardrails
- Dignified, everyday, fully clothed, diverse people; no real person's likeness or celebrity; nothing sexual, violent, frightening or humiliating; no distress beyond mild everyday frustration.
- No regulated health claim imagery (before/after bodies or health transformations, medical claims, doctors vouching, fake certifications or awards); flag it to the owner instead. Exception the owner asked for: the All-in-1 "without / with First Day" slider compares everyday moods in the same scene; keep any such pair about mood and routine, never about bodies, symptoms or measurable results (for the "worried it is not growing" door frame photo the negative may show the teen slightly lower and far fewer pencil marks, the only allowed difference besides mood).
- Every AI image is a MOCKUP: say so in the summary, keep the prompt in `PROMPTS.md`, never present it as real photography.
- If the API refuses a prompt, rephrase to a safer everyday scene; never try to bypass a refusal.

## Prompt basics and after generating
Base: "Clean bright editorial photo, soft natural window light, airy white and light blue palette with accents of noon blue (#476DED), shallow depth of field, uncluttered", plus the use ("hero background, 16:9, empty left third") and `--aspect`. With references: "Use the attached product exactly as is. Do not change its label, shape or colors." After generating: open and inspect it (no garbled text, on brand), wire it with `customImg(...)` following `firstday-design` (white page, sofia-pro, no shadows), tell the human if a JPG is over about 1.5 MB, and note in the summary that the asset is AI-generated.
