# Sources and what could not be verified

Research done on **2026-11-09** with web search and page reads. Wikipedia pages are secondary sources, Sesame Workshop's press room and "About us" are primary. Re-check dates before relying on them: the show is in the middle of a platform and format change.

- Season 57 press release (October 23, 2026): https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/press-room/sesame-street-season-57/
- Season 56 launch release (November 10, 2025): https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/press-room/sesame-streets-new-reimagined-season-launches-monday-november-10-on-netflix-and-pbs-kids/
- Sesame Street character page: https://sesameworkshop.org/our-work/shows/sesame-street/sesame-street-characters
- About us (mission, curriculum areas, 74% brand-tracking stat): https://sesameworkshop.org/about-us/
- Choosy eating printable, September 2025 (anytime and sometimes foods): https://sesameworkshop.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SW_NYP_Printables_ChoosyEating_EN.pdf
- The owner's art files: "House Style" Consumer Products Style Guide and the Specification Chart A (received 2026-11-09, outside the repo).

## Secondary (read in full or in part)
- Wikipedia: Sesame Street (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sesame_Street, read up to character offset 200000 of 286786), List of Sesame Street Muppets, Cookie Monster, Elmo, Elmo's World, Grover, Count von Count, Oscar the Grouch, Big Bird, Bert and Ernie, Abby Cadabby, Rosita, Mr. Snuffleupagus.
- Smithsonian Magazine, "Building a Great Society on Sesame Street" (2023); The Paris Review and WGA East "On Writing" (co-viewing and two-level writing, as summarized by a search); Yahoo Entertainment "Sesame Street Draws in Adults with Pop Culture Parodies" (executive producer quote, 80% co-viewing in 2019, as summarized by a search).
- Healthy Habits for Life: Penn State military families clearinghouse fact sheet (no peer-reviewed evaluations found) and press coverage of the 2005 "sometimes food" reframing (Daily Cardinal, Crimson, NPR 2008).
- Eat Brighter: Produce Marketing Association and partner reports (progress reports 2016 and 2017, A Healthier America). Internal 2007 Workshop research slides, not peer reviewed.
- Tales from 123 and Season 56: Netflix Tudum, TVLine, World Screen, a Dubbing Database fan page (voice credits).
- New characters 2020s: Sesame Workshop news ("eight times Sesame Workshop introduced new characters..."), NBC and CBS news items.
- Fan-voted catchphrase list (WatchMojo), used only for "Elmo loves you", Oscar's "Scram" and Cookie Monster's eating noise. Treated as common knowledge, not canon.

- **Dorothy the goldfish** and the **Drawer and Window** structure of Elmo's World: widely known but not in the pages read.
- **Humans of the street** (Gordon, Susan, Maria, Luis, Bob and others) and neighborhood places beyond 123, Hooper's Store and the Fix-It Shop: not read in detail.
- **Eat Brighter** current status and launch year; **Cookie Monster's Foodie Truck 2026 season** (one listing site says 2026, Netflix lists 2018): unconfirmed.
- **Tales from 123 episode titles and voice credits**: fan database, verify against official credits.
- **Emmy counts**: 76, 205 and 222 appear in different sources for different years; do not print a number without the date.
- **How the Hollywood Reporter piece and the Netflix terms describe exact episode counts** for Season 56 and 57: not stated in the releases.
- **Mama Bear's debut year**, **Lily's** introduction, and the **Sesame Street Neighborhood Adventures** details on Roblox: not confirmed.
