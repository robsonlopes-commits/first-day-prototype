# Sesame Workshop license guidelines (received 2026-11-13)

Source: the "Brand Guidelines" folder of the partnership: `FINAL_Brand, Marketing & Creative Guidelines.pptx` (24 slides: logo, characters, marketing), `SST_101_26_FirstDay.pdf` (29 pages: the characters for consumer products, art styles, "Super Tips", logo versions and don'ts, copyright formats) and three images (eyes, cropped groupings, scale chart). **This is the partner's own rulebook: it overrides every earlier guess of the skills** (`brand-rules.md` is the older, public-sources version). The studio said: guidelines "may be open to discussion between partner and Sesame Workshop", so a rule that blocks a design can be raised with them, but we build to the rules until they say otherwise.

## 1. Characters never "do business" (the big one)
Our characters always stay in character and act and talk their age, do not know they are on a TV show or famous, and:
- **cannot endorse a product, brand or partner, visually or in copy;**
- **cannot hold, wear or touch a product**;
- **cannot say the name of a product, brand or partner**;
- **cannot experience or promote a product's benefits** (no "after using X my skin is smooth", no "this gummy helps me");
- **cannot give a call to action** (buy, tune in, visit a site, "get yours").
Copy: **"do not use character voice to claim anything about the product."** Partners may not put words in a character's mouth: only their own **catchphrases** ("Elmo loves you", Cookie "Om nom nom", the Count's counting and "Ah ah ah"), **highly generic phrases** ("Happy Birthday", "Thank you!", "Hello!") and **generic phrases true to the character** ("Me love cookies", "Elmo loves to play with friends"; Cookie says "Me", never "I"). Not allowed: "I hate all desserts besides cookies" (not his tone), anything not true to character.
- Characters may not do things kids should not imitate (unsafe, unhealthy, unkind) and may not be placed in unsafe situations.
- Never write "The Muppets": always "Sesame Street" ("the Muppets of Sesame Street").
- Do not acknowledge the show, fame or fans (no "tap the Sesame Street sign" in a character's voice).
- Encouraged: hints of character personality in copy (see the bios in the PDF), lighthearted, appropriate, fun. Allowed to use the words "Sunny Day" but NO lyrics of the theme song.

## 2. Character art
- **Never change character colors** under any circumstance; **keep scale** at all times (chart: Big Bird tallest, Cookie ~2/3, Count and Ernie and Bert mid, Grover, Zoe, Elmo, Abby small: "they are to scale when their pupils are the same size"). Background characters appear smaller than foreground ones.
- **Do not flip or flop** characters with asymmetrical features: Cookie Monster (right pupil higher), the Count (monocle on his left eye), Rosita (bow on her left side). Pre-flopped versions exist in the art files for some (Cookie, Count).
- **Eyes**: never crop off the pupils; pupils never touch the edge of the eye; do not re-draw the eyes. Characters are slightly cross-eyed on purpose.
- **Cropping**: remove awkward body parts, crop at the **widest part of the body** (no bowling pin stomachs or leg stumps). Arms and legs may be cropped or covered; heads evenly spaced in a group (not stacked like a totem pole); avoid awkward tangents, odd angles, crowded groupings.
- **Peeking / posture**: characters stay right side up and connected to a surface: not floating, hovering or upside down (except daydreams).
- **One art style per project**: never mix House Style (illustration, "Furry Friends Forever"), Photography, or the variations inside a style (our Versions A, B, C). Use a single style through the entire project.
- **Compositions**: keep it clean and modern; avoid busy, cluttered compositions: dense, crowded layouts read as dated rather than timeless.
- Characters for consumer products: Elmo, Cookie Monster, Big Bird; additional favorites Abby Cadabby, Oscar the Grouch (and Slimey), Grover; broader assortments Ernie, Bert, Zoe, the Count (Count Von Count).

## 3. Logo
- **2D logo** = the primary logo for ALL activations (marketing, web, video bug). **3D logo** = packaging or special cases only, and must be paired with live-action Muppet photographs. The logo version on the product/packaging is the one to use in the marketing.
- **Never alter the logo**: no distortion or skew, no added elements, no unspecified colors, no other typography for "Sesame Street" outside the sign, no removing or changing the half circle ("123"), no old versions, no reversed coloring, no gradients, reflections, drop shadows or effects, no unapproved variations ("Sam's Street").
- **Clear space** = half the height of the horizontal portion of the logo on all sides. **Minimum size** on screen 50px wide (print 0.75 in). May sit on colored, patterned or textured backgrounds as long as they do not clash, compete or distract.
- **Lockups** with the partner's logo are allowed if the logos do not overlap and are not too close to each other.
- Versions: three color keyline (yellow border), two color green, black on yellow, one color knock-out black / white / green.

## 4. Marketing, sets, sound and approval
- **Do not recreate the set** of Sesame Street for product or lifestyle shoots (Hooper's Store, the Stoop, **the 123 street sign**). NYC-inspired set pieces as a nod are fine. Models must be diverse, appropriately dressed and wear no other brand logos.
- **No re-created voices** (no non-approved voice actors) and **no Sesame Street songs** ("Sunny Days"), original audio or character voices in any video asset (Stories, Reels, TikToks) without extra licenses and Legal approval.
- **Approval**: all campaign and photo/video shoot **concepts** are vetted by Sesame Workshop BEFORE execution; all marketing materials generated by the partner are submitted for review BEFORE posting. Allow 3 to 5 business days for marketing reviews and 7 to 10 for PR. Influencers and celebrities are vetted by Sesame Workshop (approval, yellow flag, red flag); for adult products influencers must not show children using the product, for kid-facing products children may use it.
- **Social**: because of legal restrictions (UBIT) the Sesame channels cannot promote or post partner product, CTA, co-post or share partner posts. The partner may tag the Sesame account that suits the partnership and is encouraged to use Character Birthdays, Holidays, Trend Days and Awareness Days.
- **Legal notice on ALL marketing materials** (short forms for subtle placement):
  - long (preferred on packaging and products where space permits): "Sesame Street® and associated characters, trademarks and design elements are owned and licensed by Sesame Workshop. © 2026 Sesame Workshop. All Rights Reserved."
  - medium: "TM and © 2026 Sesame Workshop. All Rights Reserved."
  - short: "TM and © 2026 Sesame Workshop."
  - shortest (very limited space only): "TM/© Sesame"
